1. User Stories
This document lists the User Stories of the GraphMind platform (GraphRAG-based Enterprise Knowledge Management Platform for SME Decision Support). Each story follows the format “As a [Role], I want [Goal], so that [Benefit]” (columns “As a/an”, “I want to...” and “so that...”) with testable Acceptance Criteria. Scope, architecture and research activities follow the Proposal Document (Version 1.1); Sprint schedule and deliverables follow the Project Plan Document (FINAL).
The platform serves exactly four system roles: SME Manager (including Executive), Standard User, Admin and Research Team. Sales, Accounting, HR, Legal and IT are department/data scopes enforced through RBAC, not system roles.
Every story belongs to one of the six modules below (a story delivered jointly by two modules, as in the Project Plan, lists both):
• Module 1 — Data Source & Sync Management
• Module 2 — Knowledge Management & Editor
• Module 3 — Hybrid AI Copilot & Search
• Module 4 — Decision Support & Alerts
• Module 5 — Enterprise Multi-facet Search
• Module 6 — System Admin & Security
Sprint schedule (Project Plan, 01/09/2026 – 01/12/2026):
• Sprint 1 — Kickoff & Planning (01/09/2026 – 22/09/2026)
• Sprint 2 — Knowledge Foundation & Fine-tuning (22/09/2026 – 20/10/2026)
• Sprint 3 — Hybrid Query Engine & Explainable Answers (20/10/2026 – 10/11/2026)
• Sprint 4 — Packaging, Benchmarking & Closing (10/11/2026 – 01/12/2026)
The last lines of each Acceptance Criteria cell state the story's Module, Sprint, Product Backlog item(s) and Jira reference. Jira IDs are quoted only where the Project Plan confirms them; otherwise “Jira ID pending” is used. Section 1.5 summarizes the traceability between User Stories and Product Backlog items.
